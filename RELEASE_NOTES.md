## BarDrop 1.17.1

* **Action bar in two rows:** each row is now centered and spread across the full width, so names like “Downloads” are no longer cut off.
* **Update button:** it just says “Update” and fits next to the BarDrop icon; the version is shown below.
* **Introduction:** long headings no longer get cut off.
* **Texts:** every text was reviewed in English and German. Some are clearer now, for example the delay (“None”, “1 second”), “Show strip right away” and the messages for resetting and updating.

**Installation:** download `BarDrop.dmg`, open it and drag BarDrop to Applications. Since version 1.10, updates come directly through the app.

> [!NOTE]
> On first launch macOS says it can’t check BarDrop. That’s only because the app isn’t notarized, not because anything was found. Allow it once under **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
